Clinical Workflow

Step-by-Step Triage & Referral Process

From image capture to doctor verification in five seamless steps.

01

Lesion Capture & Symptoms

Patient uploads a clear close-up image of the skin anomaly and completes a brief symptom history form (duration, pain, itching, site, bleeding history).

02

AI Deep Learning Analysis

Our neural network extracts pathological feature patterns, calculates class probability distributions, and evaluates image clarity.

03

Grad-CAM Heatmap Generation

The system generates visual heatmaps overlaying the original photo, highlighting exact lesion boundaries and textural cues for visual explainability.

04

Risk Stratification & Triage

Triage algorithms evaluate severity rankings and confidence margins to assign Red, Yellow, or Green urgency flags and generate a structured PDF report.

05

Specialist Review & Referral

Dermatologists access prioritized case queues, review visual heatmaps and patient symptoms, add clinical notes, and approve official referrals.

Risk Management

Tri-Color Urgency Triage Matrix

Ensuring urgent skin conditions receive immediate clinical attention while optimizing healthcare resources.

RED - HIGH RISK Target: 24 - 48 Hours

Urgent Referral Required

Assigned to severe, contagious, or potentially malignant skin conditions (e.g., Scabies, flagged suspicious lesions, low-confidence high-severity patterns).

  • ✓ High priority placement in dermatologist queue
  • ✓ Immediate patient recommendation for in-clinic review
  • ✓ Full Grad-CAM visual diagnostic breakdown
YELLOW - MODERATE RISK Target: Routine Consultation

Routine Specialist Referral

Assigned to chronic, inflammatory, or active skin disorders (e.g., Psoriasis, Candidal Intertrigo, Eczema & Dermatitis) requiring prescription management.

  • ✓ Standard clinical referral workflow
  • ✓ Recommended outpatient consultation
  • ✓ Symptom history & risk rationale summary
GREEN - LOW RISK Target: Self-Care / Follow-up

Low Risk & Primary Monitoring

Assigned to benign or mild dermatological conditions (e.g., Acne, Melasma, Vitiligo, Alopecia Areata, mild Tinea) with low risk of escalation.

  • ✓ Guidance on self-monitoring & OTC management
  • ✓ Advice to seek re-triage if symptoms persist
  • ✓ Downloadable reference report

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI classify skin lesions?

The system utilizes deep convolutional neural networks (EfficientNet) trained on dermatological image datasets to analyze skin texture, border irregularity, and visual patterns.

What is Grad-CAM explainability?

Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) produces visual heatmaps over the photo, highlighting the specific regions of interest that guided the AI's diagnostic reasoning.

How are Red, Yellow, and Green risk levels determined?

Risk tiers combine base disease severity ranks with model prediction confidence. Low model confidence automatically escalates risk levels to ensure potential anomalies receive expert review.

Can dermatologists review my case?

Yes. Submitted cases enter a dedicated clinical queue where registered dermatologists can review image heatmaps, clinical history, and attach official medical evaluations.
